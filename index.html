HERE TO HELP

How LX works.

Everything you need to get started with the creator marketplace.

How do I buy an asset?

Browse the marketplace, open a listing and choose “Get asset”. Free uploaded assets can be claimed and downloaded. Paid checkout is not connected.

How do I become a seller?

Choose “List an asset”, fill in the listing details and publish. Your listing will appear in the marketplace.

When do I receive my files?

On a live marketplace, digital files are made available once an order is complete. Free uploaded files are available after claiming them. Paid purchases are not enabled.

Can I save assets for later?

Yes. Use the heart icon on any asset card. Saved assets appear under Saved in the sidebar.
